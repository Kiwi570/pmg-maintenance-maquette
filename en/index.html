Steam, never a hammer.

An ice dam traps meltwater at the roof edge, and the water ends up seeping in: stained ceilings, dripping soffits. PMG removes it with commercial-grade steam equipment, without damaging shingles, flashing, gutters or soffits.

What never goes on your roof

  • Hammers, axes or impact tools
  • Saws or cutting tools
  • Hot water poured on the roof
  • Salt-based products

In peak season, we're often there the same day or within 24 to 48 hours, depending on demand and the weather.
